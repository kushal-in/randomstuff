## What is “Advent of Code”

**Advent of Code** , created by [Eric Wastl](https://was.tl/), is an Advent calendar of small programming puzzles for a variety of skill levels that can be solved in any programming language you like.

You don’t need a computer science background to participate – just a little programming knowledge and some problem solving skills will get you pretty far. Nor do you need a fancy computer; every problem has a solution that completes in at most 15 seconds on ten-year-old hardware.

## When does it take place?

As said before, it is an Advent calendar. It will take place over [12 days](https://adventofcode.com/2025/about#faq_num_days) (instead of the 25 days as before)

* It starts everyday **10:30 am IST** from **1st December till the 12th**.

## What is the puzzle about?

* The puzzle can be anything :​)
* All the puzzles follow a particular theme based on Christmas.
* Each puzzle has two parts, solving the first part unlocks the second.
* The puzzle does not involve submitting a piece of code to a server. Instead each participant is given a different input and they are supposed to give the expected result for the given input.

To get an idea of it have a look at the [previously conducted challenges](https://adventofcode.com/2024/events).

## How do I get started?

* It’s a free challenge available on [this website](https://adventofcode.com/).
* All you need is a place to code and a browser (and ofc, enthu :​) )
