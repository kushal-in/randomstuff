# PC Deputy Coordinator Selections 2026

Welcome to the **Programming Club Deputy Coordinator Selections 2026**.

We're excited to have you here and look forward to seeing what you can build, solve, and figure out.

## About the Role

As a Deputy Coordinator, you'll work with the Programming Club Coordinators throughout the year. The tenure will involve:

- Upskilling in **Competitive Programming and Software Development**
- Participating in programming contests and club activities
- Working with the PC team
- A few treats along the way
- Building and shipping a **mini project**

The goal is to learn, build, and get better alongside people who enjoy doing the same.

# Before You Begin

The application is intentionally **long and challenging** in some places. **You are not expected to finish everything.**

We care more about **how you approach problems** than how much you complete. Give things a genuine attempt, and don't worry about leaving difficult questions incomplete.

### A few things to keep in mind

- **Do not use AI** to solve the application. This includes ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, or similar tools. AI-generated solutions are easy to identify and defeat the purpose of the application.
- Looking up **documentation, syntax, language references, and existing reference material is completely fine**.
- **Partial solutions and reasonable attempts count.**
- Move on from problems you're stuck on and come back later.
- Don't copy or submit someone else's solution.
- If something is unclear, **ask us**.

Most importantly, **give it a try**. You don't need to be great at everything here.

# The Application

The application has two parts:

### 1. Competitive Programming
Problems covering problem-solving, algorithms, and implementation.

### 2. Software Development
Programming fundamentals, reading material, and implementation problems.

We encourage you to attempt **both parts**.

---

# Submitting Your Application

Your application is submitted **directly through this website**.

Once you verify your email:

1. Your application will be automatically sent to us.
2. You can **download a copy** of your application.
3. You can **edit and resubmit as many times as you want** before the deadline.
4. Only your final submission will be considered.

**Deadline: Sunday, October 24, 11:59 PM**

---

# Finally

Don't treat this like an exam. Some parts will be easy, and some will be deliberately difficult.

**Try things. Get stuck. Come back. Leave things incomplete if you have to.**

We care more about **how you think and approach problems** than whether you finish every question.

Good luck, and have fun.
