You probably already know the drill, we’re taking in the next batch of Deputy Coordinators for the year.

As a PC DC, you spend the year sharpening your fundamentals across CP and dev through internal sessions, and by the end of it, you build and ship a mini project from scratch. As a bonus, doing all these alongside people who like doing the same.

A few quick pointers before you start hacking away:

* Don't stress about finishing every single question. A few sections are deliberately tricky. Leaving stuff blank won’t instantly kill your chances, but we do care about how you attempt what you know.
* Write your own code. Skip ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or whatever model you usually lean on. We can usually tell, and we honestly just want to see how your head works.
* Looking up syntax, docs, or standard reference material is completely fine. That’s just normal problem-solving.
* Submissions lock on **Sunday, October 25, 11:59 PM**.

Mess around with the problems, see what you can figure out, and submit whatever you manage to put together.

Good luck kids.
